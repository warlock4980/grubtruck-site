What it does

A truck-finder built for real, hungry days.

Most apps treat food trucks like an afterthought to brick-and-mortar restaurants. We built GrubTrucks as the opposite: trucks first, mobile-first, real-time first.

📍

Open right now

The map only shows trucks that are actually serving at this moment. No more driving to a closed parking lot.

🚚

Trucks, not chains

We don't surface fast-food locations or sit-down restaurants. Just the trucks — independent, local, mobile.

🧭

One tap to drive

Pick a truck, get directions instantly via Apple Maps, Google Maps, or your favorite rideshare app.

⭐

Save your favorites

Found a truck you love? Save it. We'll surface it when it's near you, and warn you when it moves.

How it works

Three taps to lunch.

01

Open the app

The map snaps to your location. Trucks open right now appear as warm dots; closed trucks disappear from the view.

02

Tap a truck

See its menu, hours today, current location, customer photos, and how far it is from you.

03

Drive (or ride)

One tap routes you in Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, Uber, or Lyft. You're eating within fifteen minutes.

Ever stood on a street corner trying to find a food truck and ended up at a chain restaurant instead? GrubTrucks is the app that fixes that. — GrubTrucks User Manual, Page 1

For truck owners
